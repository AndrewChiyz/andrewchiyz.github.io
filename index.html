👨‍🏫 About me

I am an Associate Professor at the School of Computer Science and Technology, Guangdong University of Technology. Previously, I was a Research Fellow at Nanyang Technological University, working with Prof. Tat-Jen Cham. I received my Ph.D. from Harbin Institute of Technology, Shenzhen, supervised by Prof. Haijun Zhang.

My research focuses on computer vision and machine learning, particularly visual intelligence that perceives, represents, reasons about, and interacts with the physical world. Current interests include human-centric multimodal video analysis and synthesis, 6D object pose estimation, visual saliency reasoning and grounding, medical image segmentation, and embodied AI.
