👨‍🏫 About me

I am Yuzhu Ji, an Associate Professor and master's supervisor at the School of Computer Science and Technology, Guangdong University of Technology (GDUT), supported by GDUT's Young Hundred Talents Program (Tier A), and a Senior Member of IEEE. I received my Ph.D. from Harbin Institute of Technology in 2019, began postdoctoral research at the School of Computer Science and Engineering, Nanyang Technological University, Singapore, in 2020, and joined GDUT in 2022.

My research interests include computer vision, video analysis and synthesis, human motion transfer, object pose estimation, salient object detection, and medical image segmentation.

I have published over 40 papers in international journals and conferences in artificial intelligence, including IEEE TNNLS, TMM, TCYB, TCE, and TETCI, as well as KDD, IJCAI, ECAI, ICME, and ICASSP. I have also filed multiple invention patent applications.

I lead one Young Scientists Fund project funded by the National Natural Science Foundation of China and two General Program projects funded by the Natural Science Foundation of Guangdong Province. I also participate in one National Key R&D Program project and one General Program project funded by the Natural Science Foundation of Guangdong Province.
