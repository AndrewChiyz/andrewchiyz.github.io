👨‍🏫 About me

I am an Associate Professor at the School of Computer Science and Technology, Guangdong University of Technology. Previously, I was a Research Fellow at Nanyang Technological University, working with Prof. Tat-Jen Cham. I received my Ph.D. from Harbin Institute of Technology, Shenzhen, supervised by Prof. Haijun Zhang.

My research focuses on computer vision, with an emphasis on human-centric video analysis and synthesis, image segmentation, and medical image analysis.
