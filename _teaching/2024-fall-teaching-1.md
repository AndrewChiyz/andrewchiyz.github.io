This course takes several typical applications in the field of AI computing, and focus on the basic principles and system optimization methods involved in instruction set, compiler and software stack from software algorithm to computer architecture. To understand the design process of AI computing from the perspective of system. The purpose of this course is to develop students' basic skills in designing and using relevant compilers and software stacks and system optimization methods based on an AI computing architecture.
