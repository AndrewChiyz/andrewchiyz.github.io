Master’s students

We welcome applicants to the academic and professional master’s programmes at the School of Computer Science and Technology, GDUT, who are interested in research, R&D careers, or further doctoral study.

Research requires sustained commitment, regular paper reading, experiments, and weekly progress presentations.

Undergraduate research

Students in years 1–3 at the GDUT School of Computer Science are welcome, especially those planning postgraduate research. An introductory task and a one-to-one discussion help establish a mutual understanding before joining.

The introductory experience includes a paper presentation and reproduction of experimental results. Each task is planned over approximately one week, followed by a discussion of the paper or implementation.

Preparation & expectations

  • Linear algebra, probability and statistics, machine learning, and deep learning fundamentals.
  • Weekly progress presentations, scientific writing, and reproducible experiments.
  • Our focus is sustained academic research and high-quality publications.

Suggested starting points：Stanford CS231n；Machine Learning by Zhi-Hua Zhou; introductory courses in machine learning and linear algebra.

How to get in touch

Please email the following materials, then we can arrange an initial discussion online or in person.

  1. Your CV, including English proficiency results where applicable.
  2. Previous research or relevant project experience, if any.
  3. Research interests and future study plans.
